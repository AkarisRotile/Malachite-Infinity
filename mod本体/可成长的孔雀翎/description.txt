# 可成长的孔雀翎 · Malachite Infinity

一把会陪你成长的丛林之刃 —— 孔雀柳刃。
A jungle blade that grows with you — the Peacock Willow Blade.

========================================================
【简介】
孔雀柳刃是一把"会成长"的武器：砍树就有机会获得它。之后它会沿着一条贴近原版节奏的
成长曲线（按 Boss 推进逐档变强），配上一套独立的「念」伤害体系与「星图」天赋，陪你走完整场冒险。

从 V2.0.0 起，本模组已完全独立：它不再是灾厄（Calamity）的附属模组，不要求安装灾厄，
也不与灾厄里的"孔雀翎"互相干扰。你可以单独游玩，也可以把它当作灾厄之外的成长内容搭配使用。

[English · Introduction]
The Peacock Willow Blade is a weapon that "grows": chop down trees to obtain it, and it becomes
stronger along a growth curve paced after vanilla progression (boss by boss), backed by an
independent Mind (念) damage class and the Star Map talent system.

As of V2.0.0 this mod is fully standalone: it is no longer an add-on of Calamity, does not require
Calamity, and never conflicts with Calamity's own "Malachite" weapon. Play it alone, or stack it
on top of Calamity as extra growth content.

========================================================
【目前内容一览】 What's included

- 获取：砍树掉落孔雀柳刃（新世界需重新获取）。Obtainable by chopping trees (re-obtain on new worlds).
- 成长：WillowGrowth 独立成长曲线，伤害/攻速/护甲穿透随 Boss 进度逐档提升（贴近原版梯队）。
  Growth: the independent WillowGrowth curve scales damage / attack speed / armor penetration through vanilla boss tiers.
- 「念」伤害：独立伤害类，吃"通用/召唤"全额加成，并对"近战/远程/其它模组"按 25% 兼容。
  Mind (念): a standalone damage class — full scaling from generic/summon bonuses, 25% compatibility from melee/ranged/other mods.
- 星图（左下角✦）：「力」页五轨加点（攻击/穿甲/攻速/暴击/并发弹幕，暴击溢出增幅终伤），免费洗点；「技」页为技能预留，开发中。
  Star Map (✦, bottom-left): "Strength" page — five talent tracks (damage / armor pen / attack speed / crit / volley), free respec; "Technique" page reserved for skills (WIP).
- 技能点：不同 Boss 首杀发放。
  Skill points: awarded on first-time boss kills; every online player in the world shares in the reward.
- 近战形态原型（自定义改键）：三段斩击，命中叠「精准」，双击方向=突进斩。参数仍在实机调参中，手感确认后并入「技」页。
  Melee form prototype (F key, rebindable): three-step pure-VFX arc slashes (wind-up -> burst overshoot -> settle), "Precision" stacks on hit, double-tap directional dash slash. Tuning in progress; it will become the first "Technique" node once the feel is approved.
- 领域展开（V 键）：原型技能。 Domain Expansion (V key): prototype ability.
- 对话与剧情：少女的陪伴线
  Dialogue & story: a gentle jungle-blade spirit companion
- 潜伏：自建潜伏值，满值自动扇形齐射。
  Stealth: an independent stealth gauge; at full charge, attacks become a fan volley.
